# Phase 6B physical navigation validation

## Route behavior

The phone header displays the movement source. In **Live Sensors**, selecting a landmark or tapping an in-bounds map point sets a target. The map tap uses the inverse camera transform and mission projection, and the spherical distance from Habitat Base enforces the mission radius. A target outside the boundary leaves the previous route in place. **Start Navigation** activates guidance without changing the astronaut's position. The dashboard retains the route through sheet changes, pause, and a foreground restart of the sensors. Choosing **Demo Movement** cancels live guidance and allows the separately labeled simulation; starting it animates a demo route.

The dashed route joins the current and target Mars coordinates in the existing north-up Mars Trek projection. It updates from the last accepted position and shares the map's pan and zoom transform. The projected line is direct guidance through terrain that has not been checked for traversability. Remaining distance and distance to Habitat Base are great-circle distances on the Mars 2000 sphere. A walk away from the target can increase the remaining distance.

Return to Base becomes available on the phone when the current accepted Mars position is strictly more than 100 m from Habitat Base. A selected marker never counts toward that threshold. The comparison has a 0.000001 m floating-point margin at exactly 100 m and uses no hysteresis. Only accepted position changes can move the control across the threshold. Confirming Return to Base selects the base and starts direct guidance. Live Sensors retains the last local position and the existing pause state.

Live arrival starts with an accepted displacement. It requires a second eligible fix from a different native callback 2–8 seconds later. Each fix must report horizontal accuracy of 6 m or better. The remaining Mars distance, the follow-up fix's separation from the accepted Earth anchor if it produced no displacement, and its reported accuracy must fit inside a 12 m allowance. A close stationary fix can complete that check without moving the marker. An out-of-tolerance or rejected fix, route replacement, pause, rebaseline, sensor failure, or foreground restart clears the candidate. Arrival ends guidance, keeps the actual accepted Mars position, and displays the destination or Habitat Base message once. These are provisional thresholds for the educational simulation.

## Physical movement sources

- **iPhone 16, ARES-01.** The foreground Core Location bridge reports full-accuracy authorization, horizontal accuracy, speed, course, reported speed/course accuracy, and a callback identifier. It rejects fixes flagged as software-simulated. Core Motion supplies relative device orientation. CMPedometer can report cumulative steps and an estimated distance when Motion & Fitness permission and the hardware provide them. The number of recent device callbacks must be measured in the walking trial. [Apple Core Location](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/corelocation/cllocation) · [Apple CMPedometer](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmpedometer)
- **OPPO CPH2579, ARES-02.** Live Sensors uses the GPS provider with precise foreground permission. The bridge uses the fix's monotonic elapsed timestamp mapped to epoch time and discards network-provider fixes from live guidance. Sensor Test retains GPS and network-provider diagnostics. The step bridge uses the hardware step counter, with the step detector as fallback, subject to Physical Activity permission. It supplies steps without a measured distance field. The game rotation vector, or the standard rotation vector when needed, supplies relative device orientation. [Android location fix fields](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/location/Location) · [Android motion and step sensors](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/sensors/sensors_motion)

The Phase 5D location guard is the sole live position writer on both platforms. A usable fix is at most 15 s old and has horizontal accuracy no worse than 50 m. A first fix, or the first fix after a gap longer than 30 s, sets an Earth baseline without applying movement. The guard rejects duplicate, old-window, mock, malformed, coarse and stale fixes. A discontinuity above both 40 m and the summed uncertainty for the relevant pair holds position until explicit rebaseline. Displacement must exceed the sum of the baseline and new accuracy radii. The guard requires three progressing samples from separate callbacks at least 0.5 s apart, with at least 2 m of further progress. A candidate needs a plausible walking speed of at most 3.5 m/s, speed accuracy at most 1 m/s, speed above that error by at least 0.35 m/s, and course accuracy at most 35°. Reported course must agree with displacement to within 45°. The applied length is the measured displacement minus the two accuracy estimates. That conservative subtraction can significantly undercount a short walk.

The accepted Earth displacement vector provides east and north components. One accepted Earth meter maps to one simulated Mars meter along that bearing. GPS course corroborates the vector. The **Calibrate Arrow** control aligns the device's screen-top pose with map-up for the relative orientation glyph. It has no effect on translation, and the phone may be held or carried normally during walking. There is no stride-length calibration in this phase. Pedometer windows, duplicate handling and cumulative deltas are diagnostic evidence only. GPS and pedometer distances never accumulate together. Indoor or coarse fixes, missing speed or course, and poor direction agreement can leave **Position uncertain · Paused** throughout a real walk. Guidance stays selected and resumes as valid evidence returns.

## Firebase and reporting

The existing writer publishes Mars position, relative orientation, movement mode and quality to the configured session. It serializes writes and coalesces changes while a write is in flight. Jezero uses `missions/jezero/sessions/field-test-01`. Live guidance rejects local position restoration from Firebase. The existing demo startup restore remains available. Shared presence does not contain raw Earth coordinates or pedometer samples. Offline and reconnect processing continues through the existing connection and freshness policy.

The local Movement Log includes accepted or rejected fix evidence and applied Mars meters. Position changes and route transitions record remaining destination distance and base distance. Export privately because the opt-in report can contain exact Earth coordinates. Keep the measured course length, bearings, and start/end times separately. [Movement Log and export](live_movement_validation.md)

## Field procedure

Run the same trials separately on the iPhone and OPPO. Use a measured, unobstructed walking course with open sky. Record the actual course and the conditions for each device.

1. Sign in with the assigned mission PIN and open **Jezero Crater**. Open **Astronaut**, scroll to **Device Sensors**, and select **Live Sensors**. Verify the **LIVE SENSORS** header. Allow precise foreground location and give Motion & Fitness or Physical Activity permission if recording steps. Open **Movement Log**, tap **Start Capture**, and close it without leaving the dashboard.
2. Tap **Fit Area**. Use a named waypoint or tap inside the boundary in a direction matching the marked Earth course. Record the displayed starting distance and tap the orange navigation arrow. If the app reports position uncertain, wait for an eligible baseline. Rebaseline only while stationary and only when the log reports a hold or a new trial is starting. Record time and movement state.
3. Complete the trials below. After each trial, stop the capture and export it separately. Include device model, OS, authorization, native steps, native distance when available, fix accuracy and course quality, accepted Mars displacement, and the remaining target distance. Record hold or rejection reasons when no displacement was accepted.

| Trial | Procedure and expected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| A · Stationary | Leave the phone at one marked point for 60 s after the baseline. Verify zero accepted displacement, no arrival, and a stable remaining distance. |
| B · 20 m | Walk a measured straight 20 m in the recorded bearing and stop. Check accepted movement and confidence against the measured course. |
| C · 50 m | Repeat on a measured straight 50 m course and compare accepted distance, progress timing and stop behavior. |
| D · Turn | Walk the first measured leg, turn about 90°, and walk the perpendicular leg. Compare the direction of accepted segments, and check for a hold or jump. |
| E · Stop | Stop in place and wait for at least 15 s. Confirm that remaining distance stops changing. |
| F · Return | After validating movement, walk until the accepted Mars pose exceeds 100 m from Habitat Base. Confirm the home button is enabled, start return guidance, and check that the marker holds until new movement is accepted. Walk back along the measured course. Verify that base arrival occurs once and preserves the measured pose. |
| G · Pair | In **Crew → Join Crew**, sign in to the same Jezero session as separate astronauts. Repeat a measured walk while the other phone observes. Check the remote pose against accepted local segments and compare stale/reconnected state without switching to Demo Movement. |

## Observed results

- **OPPO CPH2579.** The Phase 6B DEV APK installed and launched. Jezero opened as ARES-02 and showed the Live Sensors status. Android's sensor service listed rotation, step counter and step detector capability. SIDERA VI requested precise location, and the app showed Earth location as unavailable before the user granted it. Physical Activity permission was also ungranted during the check.
- **iPhone 16.** The release build signed successfully with a profile covering the phone. CoreDevice's network connection reset during installation, so a Phase 6B installation and launch were unconfirmed. To complete installation, connect and unlock the phone, open Xcode's Devices and Simulators, select the iPhone, and add the signed `Runner.app` from the release build.
- **Automated coverage.** Flutter tests exercise synthetic location, route, arrival and presence inputs. Native XCTest and Android JVM tests passed. The measured stationary, walking, return and paired-device trials are pending on each smartphone.
